Kebijakan privasi

Cognify Notch dibuat supaya datamu tetap di komputermu. Halaman ini menjelaskan apa yang disimpan, apa yang dikirim ke internet, dan ke mana. Berlaku sejak 21 Oktober 2026 untuk app Mac, app Windows, dan situs ini.

Ringkasnya

  • Tidak ada akun, iklan, analitik, atau pelacak, baik di app maupun di situs ini.
  • Pembuat app tidak menerima data apa pun darimu.
  • Catatan, tugas, clipboard, dan data lain tersimpan di komputermu saja.
  • Hanya beberapa fitur yang memakai internet. Semuanya tercantum di bawah.

Yang tersimpan di komputermu

  • Catatan, tugas, minum, dan timerTermasuk jumlah sesi fokus hari ini.
  • Riwayat clipboardHingga 40 teks terakhir. Gambar hanya selama app berjalan. Isi dari pengelola kata sandi dilewati. Mati bawaan di Windows.
  • File di trayHanya lokasinya. File tetap berada di tempat aslinya.
  • PengaturanTermasuk nama kota, tautan atau file kalender ICS, dan cache lirik.

Mac: berkas pengaturan com.reinhard.cognify.notchapp.plist di folder ~/Library/Preferences. Windows: folder %LocalAppData%\io.github.reinharddev.cognify-notch dan %LocalAppData%\CognifyNotch. Untuk menghapus semuanya, hapus app lalu hapus berkas atau folder itu.

Yang tidak pernah keluar dari komputer

  • Kalender (Mac)Hanya dibaca untuk menampilkan agenda hari ini dan besok.
  • Pengingat (Mac)Hanya jika sinkron dinyalakan. Sinkron ke iPhone diatur oleh app Pengingat dan iCloud-mu.
  • Mikrofon (Mac)Suara diubah menjadi teks di Mac ini. Bila bahasa belum punya model di perangkat, perekaman ditolak, bukan dikirim ke server. Hanya teksnya yang disimpan.
  • KameraHanya untuk Cermin. Tidak direkam dan tidak dikirim.
  • Suara yang diputar (Mac)Hanya diubah menjadi tinggi batang equalizer di memori.
  • UnduhanProgres dan selesainya dibaca dari folder Downloads, tidak disimpan.

Privacy policy

Cognify Notch is built so your data stays on your computer. This page explains what is stored, what is sent to the internet, and where it goes. Effective October 21, 2026, for the Mac app, the Windows app, and this website.

In short

  • No accounts, ads, analytics, or trackers, in the apps or on this website.
  • The developer receives no data from you.
  • Notes, tasks, clipboard, and other data stay on your computer only.
  • Only a few features use the internet. All of them are listed below.

Like any website, GitHub, LRCLIB, and Open-Meteo see your IP address and the time of the request, and keep them under their own policies.

What is stored on your computer

  • Notes, tasks, water, and timersIncluding today's focus session count.
  • Clipboard historyUp to the last 40 texts. Images only while the app runs. Content from password managers is skipped. Off by default on Windows.
  • Tray filesOnly their location. Files stay where they are.
  • SettingsIncluding your city name, ICS calendar link or file, and the lyrics cache.

Mac: the settings file com.reinhard.cognify.notchapp.plist in ~/Library/Preferences. Windows: the folders %LocalAppData%\io.github.reinharddev.cognify-notch and %LocalAppData%\CognifyNotch. To remove everything, delete the app and then those files or folders.

What never leaves your computer

  • Calendar (Mac)Only read to show today's and tomorrow's agenda.
  • Reminders (Mac)Only if sync is on. Syncing to your iPhone is handled by the Reminders app and your iCloud.
  • Microphone (Mac)Speech is turned into text on this Mac. If a language has no on-device model, recording is refused instead of being sent to a server. Only the text is saved.
  • CameraOnly for Mirror. Not recorded and not sent.
  • Playing audio (Mac)Only turned into equalizer bar heights in memory.
  • DownloadsProgress and completion are read from the Downloads folder and not stored.

Permissions requested

Permissions are requested the first time a feature is used. If you decline, only that feature stops working.

macOS: Calendarsfor the agenda macOS: Remindersfor task sync macOS: Camerafor Mirror macOS: Microphone and Speech Recognitionfor voice notes macOS: Audio from other appsfor the equalizer Windows: Camerafor Mirror Windows: Notificationsfor reminders, if you allow them

This website

This site is served by GitHub Pages. There are no cookies, analytics, or ads. Fonts come from the same server. Icons are loaded from unpkg.com, and the latest version number and release notes are fetched from api.github.com, so those two services see your IP address. Your language and operating system choices are kept in your browser's local storage and are not sent anywhere.

Third-party services

GitHub (releases, updates, and this site), LRCLIB (lyrics), Open-Meteo (weather), Apple (macOS, and iCloud if Reminders sync is on), and Microsoft (Windows, the WebView2 component, and SmartScreen). Each has its own privacy policy, and system components follow your operating system's privacy settings.

Children

The apps do not collect data from anyone, including minors.

Your control

You can turn off the features that use the internet in Settings, delete local data as described above, or inspect the open source code (AGPL-3.0) on GitHub.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page is updated together with its effective date, and important changes are mentioned in the release notes.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent through GitHub Issues (Mac) or GitHub Issues for Windows.
